## Who We Are

SMU .Hack partners with Amazon Web Services (AWS) Student Builder Group and Google Developer Group, to empower students in AI, cloud, cybersecurity, and app development

## What We Do

- **Workshops** — Hands-on sessions covering AI, cloud, cybersecurity, app development, and the latest tech tools.
- **Hackathons** — Build projects, compete, and collaborate
- **Tech Talks** — Learn from alumni and industry professional
- **Real-World Projects** — Collaborate with peers to develop practical applications that have positive impacts on students.
- **Company Visits** — Tour tech offices, connect with industry professionals, and discover career opportunities firsthand.
- **Industrial Certifications** — Upskill and get certified in industry-leading certification with support and resources from seniors.

## Our Mission

To foster a vibrant tech community at SMU where students can explore, learn, and build together — regardless of their major or experience level.
